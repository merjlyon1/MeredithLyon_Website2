Bio

Meredith Lyon is a Seattle-based interdisciplinary artist whose practice explores systems of value through reconstructed currency, textiles, labor-intensive processes, and printmaking.

She holds a BFA in Fashion Design from Pratt Institute and a Master of Urban Planning from Hunter College.

Statement

My practice begins with decommissioned U.S. currency intentionally shredded by the Federal Reserve after it is retired from circulation. I reconstruct the currency by hand, transforming it through weaving, embroidery, and other textile processes in an ongoing body of work titled Making Money.

Read full statement

The hours of repetitive labor invested in each work far exceed the face value of the money itself, creating an inversion in which value is no longer guaranteed by the state but produced through time, attention, and craft.

Years spent working in public transportation and accessibility deepened my awareness of the systems through which value is measured, distributed, and administered.
