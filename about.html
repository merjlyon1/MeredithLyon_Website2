About

I am a Seattle-based interdisciplinary artist investigating systems of value, spectacle, and labor through reconstructed currency, textiles, and printmaking. I received a BFA in Fashion Design from Pratt Institute and a Master of Urban Planning from Hunter College. My work in public transportation and infrastructure informs my interest in bureaucracy, institutional systems, and the invisible structures that organize everyday life.
